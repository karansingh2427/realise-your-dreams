Check-ins

Regular conversations, not reviews

We'll sit down together at regular points to talk through how it's going. You pick the rhythm below and in your application; we can change it later.

What a check-in looks like

About 30 minutes over dinner or a walk. Four questions:

  1. What did you spend your time on?
  2. What did you learn, including what didn't work?
  3. What's the plan for the next stretch?
  4. What would help? Contacts, a sounding board, or just space.

The final check-in at the end of the runway is a reflection on the whole six months, whatever direction things went.
